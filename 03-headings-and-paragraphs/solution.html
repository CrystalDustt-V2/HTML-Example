Welcome to the home page of Riverside High School.

History

Riverside High School was opened in 1987. The first graduating class had only 35 students.

Today the school teaches more than 900 students in grades 9 to 12.

Teachers

Our teachers are the heart of the school. Each class has a homeroom teacher who follows the group for the whole school year.
